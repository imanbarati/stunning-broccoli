# Master Visual Prompt — Google Flow / Veo-style generation

Use this as the global visual direction for every clip. Append the matching individual prompt from `clip-prompts.md`.

## Project

Create an extraordinarily ambitious theatrical trailer for **A COUNTRY THAT WOULD NOT KNEEL**, a fictional 2026 epic political drama set in an alternate-history Iran.

## Visual standard

Premium blockbuster cinematic CGI and photorealistic game-cinematic rendering. Extraordinary environmental detail, immense world scale, nuanced facial performances, physically plausible destruction, layered atmospheric depth, and carefully composed large-format cinematography. Develop an original visual identity; do not reproduce assets, characters, creatures, environments, logos, or proprietary designs from existing films or games.

## Environment and geography

- Sistan deserts, wind-shaped terrain, and dramatic mountain horizons.
- Dense Persian urban architecture, contemporary streets, public squares, and old bazaars.
- The mountains of Kurdistan and the industrial landscapes of Khuzestan.
- Realistic railway stations, civic buildings, emergency clinics, and industrial complexes.
- Turquoise Persian Gulf waters, Hormuz islands, harbours, and rocky coastlines.
- Credible vegetation, weather, building materials, and natural light.

Treat real locations as geographical settings only. All depicted events are fictional.

## Cinematography

- Large-format cinematic composition.
- 2.39:1 final widescreen crop; generate in 16:9 when required by the tool.
- 24 fps motion cadence.
- Dramatic aerial cinematography, controlled tracking shots, selective handheld intimacy, and deliberate close-ups.
- Natural motion blur, subtle film grain, volumetric atmosphere, physically plausible lighting, nuanced skin texture, and detailed environments.
- Preserve readable negative space for titles to be added later.

## Visual progression

Begin with restrained desert gold, dusty earth, and cold shadows. Move into steel-grey city environments, charcoal, smoke, and carefully restrained emergency-red accents. After the moral rupture, gradually return to natural warmth. The final dawn should feel fragile and earned, not like a triumphal victory parade.

## Human realism

Every character is an individual with stable face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, and costume across shots. Crowds should look diverse and naturally composed. Acting should be subtle and credible: fear, grief, hesitation, resolve, exhaustion, and cautious hope.

## Violence and emotional tone

Show serious consequences without graphic gore, sensationalized injuries, or gratuitous violence. Keep the commander's leg injury brief and non-graphic. Do not celebrate civilian harm. The emotional center is the human cost and the choices people make under pressure.

## Audio

Use original orchestral music, restrained Persian-inspired instrumental textures, realistic environmental sound, dramatic silence, and clearly timed dialogue. Do not imitate a recognizable existing film score, song, or artist's signature style. Record dialogue separately if generated speech is inconsistent.

## Continuity

Use the same approved character references in every applicable clip. Preserve wardrobe, location, weather, time of day, props, and color progression. Do not invent additional main characters. Follow the character bible.

## Strict fiction requirements

This is fictional alternate-history cinema, not a documentary or a claim that depicted events happened. Do not use real political figures, identifiable real commanders, actual organizations, real military insignia, genuine news graphics, fabricated casualty lists, or documentary framing. Never present generated footage as real evidence.

Do not copy Avatar, Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora, or any other existing property's characters, creatures, environments, logos, or proprietary designs. Build an original photorealistic cinematic world.

## Desired result

The finished trailer should feel emotionally devastating, visually immense, suspenseful, politically complex, and ultimately human. It should end with a difficult question about legitimate civilian self-government—not with a new strongman or a revenge fantasy.
