# A Country That Would Not Kneel

**A fictional 2026 epic political-drama trailer package.**

A nationwide fictional uprising follows a catastrophic military crackdown. From Sistan to Hormuz, civilians demand change. When an armored column advances into a crowd, a young conscript refuses an order and shoots his fictional commander in the leg. His choice fractures the chain of command and forces a country to confront the question: who governs after the old order falls?

The story's answer is deliberately not a new strongman: a temporary civilian transition, representative of the country's regions, must protect people and essential services while organizing a legitimate constitutional process.

> **Fiction notice:** All characters, dialogue, military units, organizations, events, and depicted incidents in this project are fictional. The story is alternate-history political drama, not a documentary and not a claim about actual events. Real Iranian geography is used only as the setting. Do not present generated footage as real news or evidence.

## Production workflow

1. Generate and approve character reference portraits before generating story clips.
2. Generate each clip separately using the global visual prompt and its matching clip prompt.
3. Keep character references, clothing, weather, geography, and lighting continuity consistent.
4. Assemble the 14 clips in order; use the timings and sound cues in `trailer/script.md`.
5. Add titles and subtitles in the editor rather than asking the video model to render exact lettering.
6. Label the finished work clearly as fictional alternate-history cinema.

## Creative north star

This is not a revenge fantasy. It is an epic about moral rupture, the cost of state violence, the danger of a power vacuum, and the difficult work of building a representative future. The final note should be hopeful but not falsely easy.
