# Character Bible and Continuity

All characters are fictional adults. Generate approved reference images before the trailer footage and reuse those exact references in all applicable clips. Do not design any character to resemble a known actor, public figure, or real political/military person.

## Darya Mehran — 32

- **Role:** Independent photojournalist; the emotional witness connecting the regional stories.
- **Appearance:** Iranian woman, 32, shoulder-length dark hair, expressive observant eyes, natural skin texture, practical rather than glamorous styling.
- **Wardrobe:** Dark practical field jacket, plain neutral shirt, cross-body camera strap; no visible logos.
- **Props:** Compact camera and small radio.
- **Performance:** Observant, restrained, determined. Her arc moves from documenting events to recognizing the responsibility of the future.
- **Continuity:** Same jacket, hair length, camera, and facial identity in clips 01, 04, 08, 12, and 14.

## Nima Soroush — 24

- **Role:** Young conscript whose refusal becomes the moral rupture in the story.
- **Appearance:** Iranian man, 24, short dark hair, youthful but tired face, natural proportions.
- **Wardrobe:** Plain, unmarked fictional military uniform; no real national or unit insignia.
- **Performance:** Fear and moral conflict, not swagger. After the shot, he is stunned by the irreversible consequence.
- **Continuity:** Same face, haircut, uniform, and approximate dirt/weather state in clips 06, 07, 08, and 14.

## General Kaveh Radan — 55

- **Role:** Fictional military commander who embodies the brittle old command structure.
- **Appearance:** Iranian man, 55, greying hair, lined face, controlled expression.
- **Wardrobe:** Dark unmarked fictional command uniform; no real insignia, medals, or identifiable unit markings.
- **Performance:** Controlled and severe, convinced force is necessary to maintain order. Avoid cartoon-villain acting.
- **Continuity:** Same face and uniform in clips 05, 06 (radio only if visible), and 07. Injury in clip 07 is non-graphic and brief.

## Dr. Laleh Vaziri — 40

- **Role:** Emergency physician who refuses to divide patients into political categories.
- **Appearance:** Iranian woman, 40, practical tied-back dark hair, tired but composed expression.
- **Wardrobe:** Practical medical clothing and a plain coat; no fabricated organization logo.
- **Performance:** Direct, compassionate, efficient under pressure.
- **Continuity:** Same appearance in clips 10 and 14.

## Sahar — adult

- **Role:** Teacher from Sistan and a civilian assembly voice.
- **Appearance:** Fictional adult woman, grounded and expressive.
- **Wardrobe:** Practical everyday clothing suited to the environment; avoid costume stereotypes.
- **Performance:** Grief converted into civic determination.
- **Continuity:** Same face and clothing in clips 02 and 13.

## Mehrdad — adult

- **Role:** Port engineer from Hormozgan; advocates regional participation and civilian infrastructure continuity.
- **Appearance:** Fictional adult man, practical demeanor.
- **Wardrobe:** Workwear appropriate to a port engineer; no company logos.
- **Performance:** Pragmatic, thoughtful, concerned with keeping civilian systems functioning.
- **Continuity:** Same face and clothing in clip 13; may appear in regional montage only if identity remains consistent.

## Character-reference checklist

For each character, produce:
1. Neutral front portrait.
2. Three-quarter portrait.
3. Profile portrait.
4. Full-body wardrobe reference.
5. Expression sheet (neutral, worried, determined).

Use consistent lighting and a neutral background for the reference set. Keep facial identity, age, hairstyle, wardrobe, and props stable across generations.
