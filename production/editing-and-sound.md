# Editing, Sound, and Delivery Notes

## Assembly order and pacing

Use the canonical order in `trailer/script.md`: 14 clips, approximately 6 seconds each, for an 84-second trailer. Allow slight trim handles around dialogue and transitions. The rhythm should progress from silence and geography to accelerating tension, a sudden silence at the central refusal, a nationwide montage, and a cautiously hopeful final movement.

## Sound design

- **Opening:** Wind, distant radio static, restrained low-frequency atmosphere.
- **Clips 02–04:** Introduce a subtle heartbeat-like pulse and environmental crowd sound.
- **Clips 05–06:** Engine rumble, enclosed vehicle acoustics, filtered radio dialogue, controlled low-end tension.
- **Clip 07:** One brief report, then immediate near-total silence. Do not add gratuitous gunfire or battle ambience.
- **Clips 08–11:** Let silence, distant movement, room tone, and metallic echoes carry the consequences.
- **Clips 12–14:** Gradually introduce a restrained original orchestral theme, reaching an emotional but not triumphalist final swell.

Use original or properly licensed music. Do not imitate a recognizable copyrighted film score, song, or living artist's signature style.

## Dialogue and subtitles

Record dialogue separately if generated speech is unclear. Keep the lines faithful to the script. Create an English `.srt` subtitle file and optionally a Persian subtitle translation. Check names and punctuation manually. Do not rely on AI-generated lettering inside video frames.

## Typography and title card

Add the title in post-production, not in text-to-video generation:

**A COUNTRY THAT WOULD NOT KNEEL**

End card: `2026 — AN EPIC MOTION PICTURE`  
Tagline: `One country. Millions of voices. An uncertain tomorrow.`

Use a restrained cinematic typeface with strong readability.

## Color progression

- Clips 01–03: dawn gold, dusty earth, muted warm neutrals.
- Clips 04–08: steel-grey, charcoal, restrained emergency red, smoke, and dust.
- Clips 09–12: cooler neutral tones, with human warmth in the hospital and assembly chamber.
- Clips 13–14: gradual natural gold and sunrise warmth; avoid over-saturated victory imagery.

## Editorial and visual quality control

Reject or repair clips with inconsistent faces, costume changes, duplicated crowd people, distorted hands, impossible vehicles, incoherent architecture, flickering props, unreadable pseudo-text, or excessive camera movement. Check that the commander's injury remains non-graphic. Confirm there are no actual political insignia or identifiable public figures.

## Export guidance

- Preferred: 3840×2160 master if rendered source quality supports it; otherwise use the highest native resolution.
- Frame rate: 24 fps.
- Aspect ratio: 2.39:1 final cinematic crop; retain a 16:9 safe version for social platforms if desired.
- Audio: stereo master; preserve dialogue intelligibility and avoid clipping.
- Deliverables: master trailer, social-safe crop, English subtitles, optional Persian subtitles, poster/thumbnail, credits/license notes, and production log.

## Publication disclosure

Identify the project as a fictional alternate-history drama in the video description and/or end matter. Never present generated scenes as real news footage, eyewitness documentation, or proof of actual events.
